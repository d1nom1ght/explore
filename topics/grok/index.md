Grok is a conversational AI chatbot developed by xAI, the AI company founded by Elon Musk. It is integrated into the X (formerly Twitter) platform and has access to real-time information through X posts. Grok is built on xAI's proprietary Grok LLM series and is designed to be witty, curious, and willing to tackle edgy or unconventional questions. The model is available via a subscription to X Premium+.
